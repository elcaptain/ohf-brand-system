# ESPHome: logo

Source: Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), pp. 80-87, file `Brand_Guidelines__V03-Aug26__1.pdf`. Imported 2026-09-30. The source is signed off by the board (`decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`). The source states that layout, color variations, typography, and primary palette are the same for every project, and that the Home Assistant rules apply. This file records what is specific to ESPHome and points to `projects/home-assistant/design/logo.md` for the rest.

## Anatomy
- **Logomark.** The Open Home Foundation house shape as an enclosure, in blue, with a stylized "E" drawn as a PCB antenna trace, for software that lives inside hardware to give it wireless connectivity. No dark blue foundation "legs".
- **Logotype.** "ESPHome" in Biotif semibold, Title Case (not capitals).

## Files
The ESPHome logo comes in the variants listed under Files in `projects/home-assistant/design/logo.md`, with the same uses. There is no wordmark. Files: https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/brand-assets/tree/main/esphome/logo, prefix `ESPH-`, for example `ESPH-lockup-main-color-on-light.svg`.
