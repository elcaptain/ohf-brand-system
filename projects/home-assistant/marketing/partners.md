## Partner copy
What the partner guidelines tell partners about writing for this voice (Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), pp. 124-127). This is a summary handed to partners, not a rule set: `brand/voice.md`, `brand/style.md` and `brand/messaging.md` govern, and where this section disagrees with them, they win. Paraphrased from the source; the source's own wording contains words the validator bans (see `decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`).

| Primary audience | Message focus | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Smart home users, from DIY enthusiasts and tinkerers to general consumers looking for privacy, choice, and sustainability. | Privacy, choice, sustainability, and local control. Home Assistant's mix of capability and flexibility, with human-centered design, and user-friendly options (Home Assistant Green). | Thoughtful and accessible: no "corporate committee" feel, with a focus on the positive possibility of smart tech. |

- Do: Warm, simple, human-centered language. Comfort, control, and peace of mind.
- Avoid: Corporate jargon, or selling through urgency.
