# Music Assistant: partners

## Who may use the brand
Commercial partners of the Open Home Foundation, for co-branded products, packaging, and written content. Everything carrying our branding needs final approval from the Open Home Foundation before it ships (source, p. 5). Rules: `core/standards/co-branding.md`, `core/standards/product-packaging.md`, `core/standards/product-photography.md`. TODO: integrators and non-commercial partners; the approval route in practice.

Follow `core/standards/co-branding.md`: like paired with like, optical balance, clear space at least the height of the logotype or logomark. ## Partner copy
What the partner guidelines tell partners about writing for this voice (Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), pp. 124-127). This is a summary handed to partners, not a rule set: `brand/voice.md`, `brand/style.md` and `brand/messaging.md` govern, and where this section disagrees with them, they win. Paraphrased from the source; the source's own wording contains words the validator bans (see `decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`).

| Primary audience | Message focus | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| People who want to consolidate, manage, and stream music from many sources. | Streaming, versatility, and sound quality. One unified media experience. | Curated and smooth: elegant language for the simplicity of the experience and the breadth of support. |

- Do: The quality of the experience and consolidation.
- Avoid: Technical setup difficulties, or complicated language.
