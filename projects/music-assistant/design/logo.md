# Music Assistant: logo

Source: Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), pp. 88-95, file `Brand_Guidelines__V03-Aug26__1.pdf`. Imported 2026-09-30. The source is signed off by the board (`decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`). The source states that layout, color variations, typography, and primary palette are the same for every project, and that the Home Assistant rules apply. This file records what is specific to Music Assistant and points to `projects/home-assistant/design/logo.md` for the rest.

## Anatomy
- **Logomark.** The Open Home Foundation house shape as an enclosure, in blue, with negative space forming the initials: three vertical bars as an abstracted "M" and two slanted bars as an "A", which also read as vinyl records on a shelf. No dark blue foundation "legs".
- **Logotype.** "Music Assistant" in Biotif semibold, Title Case (not capitals).
