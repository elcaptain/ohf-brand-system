# Open Home Foundation: typography

Source: Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), file `Brand_Guidelines__V03-Aug26__1.pdf`, pp. 17 and 24-27. Imported 2026-09-30. The source is signed off by the board (`decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`).

## Families
| Role | Family | Fallback | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| logotype, headings, body in print, packaging, marketing collateral | Biotif | Figtree (headings), Instrument Sans (body) | https://www.fontspring.com/fonts/degarism-studio/biotif (licensed) |
| headings and secondary copy where Biotif is unavailable | Figtree | sans-serif | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree |
| body and tertiary copy where Biotif is unavailable | Instrument Sans at 99% width | sans-serif | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans |

Biotif is not used for user interfaces or website content; the source says it is not optimized for screens. The source does not say outright that Figtree and Instrument Sans are the web faces, only that they replace Biotif "where Biotif is unavailable". Confirm.

## Usage
- Logotype: Biotif ExtraBold for "OPEN HOME", Medium for "FOUNDATION".
- The organization name in the logotype is always capitals: OPEN HOME FOUNDATION. This is a logotype rule, not a writing rule; in running text follow `brand/style.md`.
- Biotif OpenType features on: ligatures, Stylistic Alternates sets 2 and 3 (curled "t" and single-storey curled "g").
- Instrument Sans at 99% width (`font-stretch: 99%` on the variable font).
- Headings in OHF Dark Blue, body in OHF Dark Grey on light backgrounds and OHF Grey on dark (`color.md`).
