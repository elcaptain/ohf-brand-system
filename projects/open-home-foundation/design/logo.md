# Open Home Foundation: logo

Source: Marketing and Product Guidelines for our commercial partners, v1.1 (August 2026), file `Brand_Guidelines__V03-Aug26__1.pdf`, pp. 14-23 and 47-49. Imported 2026-09-30. The source is signed off by the board (`decisions/2026-09-30-import-partner-guidelines-v1-1.md`).

## Anatomy
- **Logomark.** A house: a pitched-roof outline with no gable, chimney, or other detail, on a rectangular platform with two solid dark blue "legs" for the foundation. The house shape is the enclosure in every project logomark; the legs appear only in this one.
- **Logotype.** "OPEN HOME FOUNDATION" in Biotif, all capitals. "OPEN HOME" ExtraBold; "FOUNDATION" smaller, Medium weight, in OHF Dark Blue to match the legs.
- Logotype aligned left by default.

## Files
| Variant | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Main lockup | `open-home-foundation/logo/<screen or print>/lockup/main/` | Default. Certified and trademark-registered. Use whenever in doubt. |
| Stacked lockup | `.../lockup/stacked/` | Internal design use only: right alignment, or not enough horizontal space. |
| Inline lockup | `.../lockup/inline/` | Wide, shallow spaces such as a website navigation bar. |
| Logomark alone | `.../logomark/` | Social avatars, or third parties who publish the mark only, and only where the name appears nearby. |
| Colored, light background | files ending `-color-on-light` | Default and official. |
| Colored, dark background | files ending `-color-on-dark` | Dark backgrounds only. |
| Monochrome black or white | files ending `-monochrome-on-light` or `-monochrome-on-dark` | Photographic backgrounds, or print without color. |

Paths are in the brand assets repository, https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/brand-assets/tree/main/open-home-foundation/logo; file prefix `OHF-`. Layout and naming: `core/standards/co-branding.md`, Required elements.

## Clear space and size
- Exclusion zone on every side: one third of the width of the logomark.
- The logo is drawn on a square modular grid; do not change its proportions. Other graphic elements around it should sit on the same grid.
- TODO: minimum size for print and screen. The source does not give one.

## Backgrounds
- Light: colored version (default).
- Dark: colored version for dark backgrounds.
- Photographic: monochrome black or white, whichever reads.
- Any background: must give contrast (see Misuse).

## Misuse
- Distorting, rotating, resizing, or moving any element.
- Replacing solid color with an outline.
- Placing on a background without contrast.
- Changing any color or adding a gradient. Only the palette in `color.md`.
- Drop shadows.
- Changing letter spacing.
- The logotype without the logomark. Never.
- Deprecated versions. The source shows them as images; file them here once the marketing team names them. TODO.

## Co-branding
Conforms to `core/standards/co-branding.md` and, on packaging, `core/standards/product-packaging.md`. Commercial partners may use the "A commercial partner of the Open Home Foundation" badge instead of this logo; the rules are in the co-branding standard.
