# Open Home Foundation: accessibility

Every output passes these.

## Contrast
AA for body text; see color.md contrast table. The partner guidelines v1.1 (p. 46) state that every palette is tested for contrast and color-blind safe ("no conflicts found" in their simulator). That is the source's claim; no test output is attached. Two of its pre-approved pairings for this palette fall below the WCAG 3:1 minimum for large text; `color.md` flags them. The board-approved verdicts are the rule; for outputs that must meet WCAG, do not use the flagged pairings for the uses they fail.
